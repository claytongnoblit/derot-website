## The locked basics

**Tagline:** Regulate, don't restrict.
**Hero headline:** Your apps wind you up. DeRot brings you back down.
**Category:** Digital wellbeing / nervous-system regulation — never "screen time control."

## Lead with the felt experience, not the mechanism

Consumer-facing copy leads with how it *feels* (calmer, shoulders drop, head clears, the wired feeling fades) — not the clinical explanation of why it works.

**Avoid in user-facing copy:** "vagus nerve," "heart rate," "nervous system reset," "it's physiology," biology jargon generally, "breathing gate," "strict."

Physiology is the internal reason DeRot works — real, and fine to know — but it's not the marketing line anymore. If you see "It's not woo, it's physiology" or similar in older docs, treat it as retired.

**Approved core terms:** "calm reset," "calming breath exercise," "regulate, don't restrict."

## Block vs. lock vocabulary (locked 2026-07-18)

- DeRot *can* say it "blocks" apps. A block is the breathable pause — always escapable by breathing. Fine to say "a complete block until you've breathed" even at the strictest setting.
- DeRot *never* "locks." A lock implies shutting apps off with no way through, which DeRot doesn't do. Say "your apps won't be locked."
- "Unblock" = the blocked-app sense ("Breathe to unblock it"). "Unlock" is reserved for the feature sense ("Pro unlocks…") and Emergency Unlock.

## Say this / not this

| Say this | Not this |
|---|---|
| "Your apps wind you up. DeRot brings you back down." | "Beat your phone addiction." |
| "No willpower needed." | "Take back control / crush your screen time." |
| "We meet you mid-scroll." | "We block distractions." |
| "Feel calmer on the other side." | "Use your phone less." |
| "Never locked out of your apps." | "Not another screen time blocker." |

Rule of thumb: if a line could appear on a hard-blocker's landing page, it's wrong for DeRot.

## Voice

Calm, warm, grown-up, lightly knowing, gently self-aware. Talk to the user as a capable adult, never a patient or an addict. Not clinical, preachy, alarmist, hustle-y, or guilt-trippy. Shipped copy avoids em-dashes (commas/colons/periods instead).

## The mechanic (locked 2026-06-30)

DeRot interrupts **mid-scroll** — after the feed has already wound you up, not before you open it. There is deliberately no "breathe before you open" gate. Always describe the trigger as "DeRot pauses your scroll to bring you back down," never "breathe before you open."
