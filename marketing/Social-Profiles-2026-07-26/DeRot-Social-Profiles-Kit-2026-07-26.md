## Voice reminder (from the positioning brief)

Calm, warm, grown-up, lightly knowing — reassuring and plain-spoken, never clinical, preachy, alarmist, or guilt-trippy. Rule of thumb: if a line could appear on a hard-blocker's landing page ("beat your phone addiction," "take back control"), it's wrong for DeRot.
