InstagramTue Oct 20, 23:30 UTCresetqueuedw2-tue-resets
Three resets, three moments. Save this one for later.

Calm breathing (in for 4, out for 6) is the everyday reset. It's DeRot's default because it's the easiest one to do without thinking.

Box breathing gives a busy mind something to count. 4-7-8 is slower and works well when you're trying to wind down at night.

There are free timers for all three at derot.org/tools. If any pattern makes you dizzy, stop and breathe normally.

DeRot brings these into the apps that wind you up. Coming soon to iPhone. Launch list: link in bio.

ThreadsTue Oct 20, 21:00 UTCresetqueuedw2-tue-box
Box breathing, for when your head is buzzing:

In for 4. Hold for 4. Out for 4. Hold for 4.

Four rounds is about a minute. If the holds feel like too much, shorten them to 2. There's a free timer here:
https://derot.org/tools/box-breathing-timer/
