## LinkedIn
Most advice on news anxiety says to consume less news. The research points to something more specific: repeated, fragmented coverage seems to keep the body on alert. After the Boston Marathon bombings, people who took in six or more hours a day of coverage reported more acute stress than people who were directly exposed.

There's also a checking loop. Researchers describe consuming more news to ease a sense of threat, which tends to pull people further in. That's why "just checking one thing" so often turns into 20 tense minutes.
