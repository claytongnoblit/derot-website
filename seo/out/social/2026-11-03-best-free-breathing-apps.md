## LinkedIn
Search for free breathing apps and you mostly find free downloads with a subscription waiting inside. So we compared five popular options by what you get without paying: free tier, paywalled features, techniques, account, platforms, and price as listed, checked 3 November 2026.

The short version: Apple's Mindfulness app (Breathe) is fully free on Apple Watch, The Breathing App has a free core with no account and no ads, and iBreathe is free with ads. Calm and Breathwrk keep most of their value behind in-app purchases.

The research is reassuring here too. A 2023 Stanford trial used just 5 minutes a day of simple breathing, so a plain pacer is enough for most people. We make DeRot, an app in this category, and kept it out of the ranking.
