The calming part of a breath isn't its size. It's mostly the pace.

Slow breathing at about six breaths per minute reliably raises heart rate variability, a marker of your rest-and-digest system. A longer exhale seems to add to how relaxed it feels.

Try it after a scroll that wound you up: in through the nose for 4, out for 6, three times. Keep each breath small.
