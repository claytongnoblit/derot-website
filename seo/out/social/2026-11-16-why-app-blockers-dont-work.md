## LinkedIn
Many people install an app blocker, tap past it within days, and then feel worse about it. The research suggests a reason: friction and self-set limits reliably cut app opens and minutes, but social media breaks show small or no effects on well-being.

Blockers manage access. What often sends people back to the feed is their state, the wound-up, restless feeling the feed itself helps create. Interestingly, one friction study found the easy option to dismiss worked better than a message asking people to deliberate.
