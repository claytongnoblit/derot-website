Most advice on late-night scrolling is about rules: keep the phone out of the bedroom, stop at a hard cutoff. That works for some people. Many of us still end up in bed with a tight jaw and a slightly quick heartbeat.

## Short video script (TikTok / Reels / Shorts, faceless)
[0-2s] Text: Scrolling in bed and your heart's a little quick?
[2-6s] VO: That's not a willpower problem. Charged content keeps your mind switched on.
[6-11s] VO: Research suggests how invested you are matters more than how long you scroll.
[11-15s] VO: So don't lecture yourself. Bring yourself back down instead, right where you are.
[15-26s] Text + VO: In through the nose for 4. Out slowly for 6. Six rounds, about one minute.
[26-30s] VO: Then turn the phone face down. Let one more slow exhale end the day.
