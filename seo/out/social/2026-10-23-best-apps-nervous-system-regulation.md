## LinkedIn
Search for nervous system regulation apps and you get a lot of "vagus nerve reset" marketing with very little method behind it. So we put together a neutral comparison: Apple Mindfulness, Breathe2Relax, Breathwrk, HeartMath and Settle, each described with the same fields and with prices dated to October 23, 2026.

The evidence sorts these apps more clearly than feature lists do. A meta-analysis of 223 studies found slow breathing raised vagally mediated HRV, and an HRV biofeedback meta-analysis found large drops in self-reported stress. None of the apps has its own outcome trials, so the method matters more than the brand.

We also asked a question most roundups skip: when do you actually use the app? Most of them assume a planned, calm moment. DeRot makes an app built for the mid-scroll moment, and we kept it out of the ranking. https://derot.org/blog/best-apps-nervous-system-regulation/
