How to stop doomscrolling without quitting anything: watch your body first. Tight jaw. Shoulders up. Shallow breath. Rereading the same bad news.

That's your cue. Thumb off the screen, three slow breaths (in for 4, out for 6, about 30 seconds), then decide on purpose: keep going, switch, or close the app.

Most advice on doomscrolling is about minutes: set limits, delete apps, try harder. But doomscrolling is really a state. It's the point where the feed has wound you up and you keep scrolling anyway.

The research helps explain why feeds hold on so well. In a study of about 105,000 headline tests, each extra negative word raised clicks by about 2.3%. Research on pause apps suggests that a short pause plus a real choice to stop does more than a timer alone. So the useful move is a 30-second reset mid-scroll: in for 4, out for 6, three times, then choose on purpose.
