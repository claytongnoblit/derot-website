## Threads
The physiological sigh comes in two doses, and most advice mixes them up.

Quick reset: 1 to 3 sighs. That's a full inhale through the nose, a small top-up, then a slow exhale through the mouth. About 30 seconds.

Daily practice: about 5 minutes of cyclic sighing. That's the only dose the 2023 Stanford trial tested.

## LinkedIn
"How long should I do a physiological sigh?" has two answers. For a quick reset, one to three sighs, which takes about 10 to 30 seconds. For a daily practice, about five minutes of cyclic sighing.

Only the second dose has been studied. The 2023 Stanford trial in Cell Reports Medicine tested five minutes a day for 28 days. Mood improved more than with mindfulness, but anxiety fell about equally across groups, and sleep and HRV did not change. Single sighs weren't tested at all.

In practice, that means a few gentle sighs when a feed has left you wired, and a timed five minutes if you want a daily habit. We wrote up the dosage, the stopping signs and the safety notes. DeRot, our iOS app that is launching soon, is built for that mid-scroll moment.
