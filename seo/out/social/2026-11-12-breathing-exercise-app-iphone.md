Most roundups of breathing apps for iPhone sort them by star rating. We compared seven apps on what actually matters on an iPhone: Apple Watch support, Apple Health logging, Lock Screen and Control Center widgets, breathing patterns, and the price as listed on the App Store.

A few findings surprised us. Apple's built-in Breathe lives in the free Mindfulness app on Apple Watch and lets you set the pace, but its listing doesn't mention inhale-to-exhale ratios or holds. Breathwrk's US listing says "Designed for iPhone" and requires iOS 26. And none of the listings we checked names the physiological sigh.
