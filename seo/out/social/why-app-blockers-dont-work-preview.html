Ever tapped "Ignore Limit" and felt a little worse afterward? That isn't a failure of self-control. Blockers manage minutes, but what often sends us back to the feed is how we feel: wound up, restless, wanting relief.

Blockers do work for cutting use, and if you want firm limits for work or sleep they're still a good tool. They're just less reliable at changing your mood.

Another option: treat the limit screen as a cue to come back down. Phone face down, one long sigh, then in for 4 and out for 6, three rounds. About 30 seconds. Stop and breathe normally if you feel dizzy.
