4-7-8 breathing has a number, and it's 4. Four cycles at the full count take about 76 seconds. After about a month you can go up to 8 (roughly 2.5 minutes), and that's the ceiling.

If the 7-second hold leaves you lightheaded, stop, then try the fast count: in 2, hold 3.5, out 4. It keeps the ratio and finishes in under 40 seconds.

## LinkedIn
Most 4-7-8 breathing guides explain the technique without telling you how long to do it. The answer is 4 cycles. At 19 seconds per cycle, that's about 76 seconds, and the original guidance from Dr. Andrew Weil allows up to 8 cycles only after about a month of practice.

The 7-second hold is the part that makes people lightheaded. The fix isn't to push through. Do fewer cycles, use the faster 2-3.5-4 count (same ratio, half the time), or switch to 4-6 breathing, which has no hold. In a 2025 study of 84 people, 4-6 breathing actually raised heart rate variability more than 4-7-8.
