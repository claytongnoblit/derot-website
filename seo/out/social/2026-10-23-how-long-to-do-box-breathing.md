## LinkedIn
Most guides answer "how long should you do box breathing?" with a vague "a few minutes." The math is more useful: one 4-4-4-4 round takes 16 seconds, so four rounds is about a minute and five minutes is about 18 to 19 rounds.

The research fits that split. In a 2026 pilot of 47 students, one minute of box breathing during real anxious moments reduced state anxiety more than a passive control. In the 2023 Stanford trial of 5 minutes a day, mood gains grew with the number of days people practiced, though box breathing did not significantly beat mindfulness and HRV did not change.
