## LinkedIn
Screen time guilt is usually a reaction to a number. What most adults actually feel after a long scroll is a comedown: a tight jaw, raised shoulders and shallow breathing. In research on parents, guilt about screen time predicted stress a month later, while hours of use had little link to either.

That suggests a better question than "how many minutes?" Ask "how do I feel when I put it down?" It's answerable in the moment and points at something you can change. Studies on self-compassion point the same way: being kinder to yourself after a setback is linked to more motivation to improve, not less.
