## LinkedIn
4-6 breathing is about as simple as breathwork gets: in through the nose for 4 counts, out for 6, no breath holds. At roughly 10 seconds per breath, six breaths take about a minute.

We wrote a practical guide to it, including a breath-by-breath 60-second script for the moment you notice a scroll session has left you tense, the common mistakes (gulping the inhale, pushing the exhale, counting too fast), and a 3-5 version if 6 counts feels too long.

We also look honestly at the research: slow breathing has the clearest support, while the longer exhale seems to mainly make it feel easier and more relaxing. https://derot.org/blog/4-6-breathing-how-to/
