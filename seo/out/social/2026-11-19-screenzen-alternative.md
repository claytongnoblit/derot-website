## LinkedIn
ScreenZen adds a wait before you open an app. For many people that wait works for a few weeks, then turns into something they tap through. A 2023 PNAS study of the similar app one sec measured this: the share of opens people dismissed fell from 43% in week 1 to about 32-34% by weeks 4-6. It also found the option to back out did more than the delay itself.
