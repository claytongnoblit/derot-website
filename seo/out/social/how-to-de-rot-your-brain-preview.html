Brain rot is internet slang, not a diagnosis. But the foggy, wired feeling after a long scroll is real, and you don't have to quit your apps to come back down from it.

Start with a quick check: jaw, shoulders, breath. If two of the three are tight, set the phone face down and breathe in through your nose for 4, out slowly for 6, for about six rounds. Then rest your eyes on something far away for about 40 seconds. Stop if you feel dizzy.

DeRot (coming soon to iOS) pauses your apps while you reset with a few calming breaths.
