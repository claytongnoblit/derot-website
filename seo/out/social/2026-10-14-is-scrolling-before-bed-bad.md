Most advice about phones and sleep opens with "it's the blue light" and ends with "ban your phone from the bedroom." The research is more interesting than that. A 2024 panel of 16 experts reached consensus for kids and teens, but not for adults.

When you separate the mechanisms, light looks modest for most people at normal phone brightness (though sensitivity varies a lot), and Night Shift made no difference in a 2021 study of 167 young adults. The bigger factors seem to be a later bedtime and content that leaves you activated.
