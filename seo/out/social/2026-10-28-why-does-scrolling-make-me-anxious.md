## LinkedIn
Plenty of people pick up their phone to check one thing and surface 20 minutes later with a tight jaw and a vague sense of dread. The research points to content and posture more than minutes: in 2021 experiments, 2 to 4 minutes of COVID news lowered positive mood, while kindness stories did not.

The evidence is honest about its limits. Most of it is correlational, the effects are modest, and anxious people may also scroll more. But the felt state is real, and it's a useful signal.

One small fix: when you notice it mid-scroll, take five breaths of inhale 4, exhale 6. We wrote up the why and the how, and it's the moment DeRot is being built for. https://derot.org/blog/why-does-scrolling-make-me-anxious/
